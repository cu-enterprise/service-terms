Privacy policy - cu-e.ai

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

cu-e.ai, Global, Global. Contact: lab.cue.ba@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you message us on WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram or our website chat: your name and number or account ID as the channel shows it, and the messages you send.
  • When you fill in a form on our pages: first and last name, email, and, if the form asks, phone, country and time zone.
  • Your choices: whether you agreed to receive updates, the exact wording you agreed to, and the time, IP address and browser it was given from.
  • Registrations and payments: what you registered for, its status and the amount. Card details are handled by Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Page visits: a random visitor ID kept in your browser, device type (mobile or desktop), the website you came from, and campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source).

Why we use it

To answer your messages, handle your registration and payment, send you what you asked for (confirmations, payment links), send updates only if you agreed to them, and see which pages and campaigns work.

How we answer messages

An assistant running on our own servers drafts or sends replies; a person takes over when needed. Your messages are not used to train outside AI services.

Who else processes it

Meta (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram) to carry the messages; Stripe for payments; Cloudflare to host our pages; our email provider to send mail. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

As long as needed for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete it. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. You can stop updates at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email, or by telling us.

Delete your data

Email lab.cue.ba@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the email address or phone number you used, or send "delete my data" in any chat with us. We confirm within 30 days. Payment records we must legally keep are kept; everything else is deleted.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above.
